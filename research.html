多模态大模型对齐研究Multimodal LLM Alignment Research

CUHK MMLab · Prof. Xiangyu Yue

Feb – Nov 2025
自主提出研究方向，与 OneLLM 论文一作就对齐方法与实验设计进行早期讨论，确定以隐藏层表征分析为切入点。Independently proposed research direction; discussed alignment methodology with the first author of OneLLM, settling on hidden-representation analysis as the entry point.
修改 OneLLM 代码库，挂载并导出全部 32 层多模态 Transformer 的隐藏状态；计算余弦相似度与正负样本裕度曲线（100 张图 × 5 条 caption，bootstrap 95% CI）。Modified OneLLM codebase to hook and export hidden states across all 32 transformer layers; scripted cosine-similarity and positive–negative margin curves (100 images × 5 captions, bootstrap 95% CI).
发现跨模态判别能力在中间层（15–22）达到峰值而非随深度单调提升，说明对齐信息集中于中层而非最终输出层。Found cross-modal discriminative ability peaks at middle layers (15–22) rather than rising monotonically with depth, indicating alignment information concentrates mid-network rather than at the output layer.
